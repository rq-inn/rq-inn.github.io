What You Can Do

Use maps as a play surface, not just a picture.

GloranthaGIS turns geography into something the whole table can read and act on.

Place Jump

Search place names in Japanese or English and jump straight to the location.

Distance Measurement

Draw routes on the map and see how far journeys really are.

Range Circle

Visualize travel range, territory, and effect areas with radius tools.

Layer View

Load PNG maps and inspect the world with zoom, drag, and layered viewing.

Distance Origin Flag

Set a blue reference flag and read north/east offsets from the screen center.

Built For Play

Use it during sessions to make movement, geography, and travel feel concrete.

How to Start

Get running in three steps.

1

Get the Map

Explore And Play
